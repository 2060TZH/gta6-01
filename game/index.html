SOLMAR BAY
A sun-soaked open-world crime game
Mara Voss came back to Solmar Bay with $500 and a grudge. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay PD, run six jobs for the crew — and own the city that owes her everything. An original game: its own city, characters, story, art, sound and music.
On foot: WASD move · Shift run · Mouse look (click to lock) · E enter nearest car · F punch · Click shoot pistol · R reload
Driving: W accelerate · S brake / reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake · E get out
World: Steal cars, dodge traffic, collect 20 hidden gold packages (+$100 each), drive a taxi for fares. Wanted stars bring the police — break line of sight to cool off. Getting busted or wasted costs $300 and restarts the current job.
Save: progress auto-saves after every mission, package, taxi fare and pause; Continue resumes ammo, health, money, wanted level, mission stage, packages and your car.
